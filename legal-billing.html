2. Secure payments and service scope

Approved fixed-price packages show their INR total, applicable tax component, work scope, exclusions, and timeline before the customer opens secure Razorpay checkout. The customer must approve the written terms. Custom work begins as a free quotation request; no custom-project payment is collected before a final quote is agreed. Prepaid 30-day packages do not auto-renew. For international projects, a customer can request a no-obligation written quote. Freshtiq will disclose the final INR payable amount, exclusions, accepted international-card method and applicable tax treatment before the customer authorises payment. Local SAR/AED estimates are indicative, not direct foreign-currency charges. No customer is asked to resolve our tax classification.

3. Payment receipt versus GST tax invoice

After the gateway confirms captured payment, customers receive a private receipt and tracking reference. This payment acknowledgement is not itself a statutory GST tax invoice. The invoice requires verified customer billing information, correct service SAC, place of supply, CGST/SGST or IGST where applicable, sequential invoice numbering, and any applicable e-invoicing or export requirements. Where required by GST law, advance receipt-voucher obligations must also be assessed. Customers may submit billing details from the private order receipt; invoices are issued only after compliance review, without a second payment.

4. Refunds, cancellation and delivery

See our Refund Policy and Delivery & Handover for how requests, refunds, milestones and delivery prerequisites are handled. Commencing work does not remove legally protected customer remedies. Additional third-party charges cannot be added without prior approval.

5. Privacy and grievances

Customer billing and contact records are used for service fulfilment, reconciliation and lawful tax documentation. Our Privacy Policy explains personal-data processing and requests. For an order complaint, refund dispute, data request or billing concern, write to hello@freshtiqautomation.com with your order reference. We aim to acknowledge complaints promptly, and resolve them within applicable legal timelines. A named Grievance Officer will be published after formal designation; this page does not invent a person's name.

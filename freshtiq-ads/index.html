Beta status, partner commissions and advertising safety

Current capability: free campaign planning, independent official partner links and Freshtiq CRM enquiry capture. Not currently available: one-click ad publishing inside Freshtiq, verified affiliate payouts, customer ad wallets or automated third-party payments.

HilltopAds link above includes Freshtiq's account-issued referral ID. Standard advertiser and publisher referrals are generally 5% of HilltopAds' profit on active referrals; a separate higher-rate blogger tier has eligibility requirements. TikTok link is informational only and not affiliate-tracked. No earnings arise from simply sending an enquiry; provider verification and activity determine eligibility.

Advertising must comply with provider policies and applicable laws. Restricted or prohibited goods, false claims and illegal offers cannot be promoted. Results, low-cost traffic, customer orders and profit are never guaranteed.

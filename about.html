Operating principles

What should stay consistent across every project.

Outcome before tooling

We start from the bottleneck and choose technology after the workflow is understood.

Human approval where needed

Commercial, payment, legal and other sensitive decisions can remain explicitly human-controlled.

Truthful proof

Internal builds, prototypes and client work are not presented as if they were the same kind of evidence.

Documented ownership

Scope, integrations, dependencies, handover items and recurring provider costs are confirmed in writing.

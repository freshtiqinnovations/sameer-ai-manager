Our Story

FRESHTIQ INNOVATIONS (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED was founded with a single mission — to make advanced technology accessible to every Indian business, regardless of size or budget. Starting from a small workshop in Azamgarh, we have grown into a trusted name in AI automation, software development, and digital transformation.

Our brand, Freshtiq Automation, represents our commitment to delivering fresh, innovative, and high-quality automation solutions. Whether it's a kirana store in Delhi needing inventory management or a tech startup in Bangalore requiring AI chatbots, we bring enterprise-grade solutions to businesses of all sizes.

Registered as a One Person Company (OPC) under the Companies Act, 2013 with CIN: U47912UP2025OPC236875, we are fully compliant with Indian regulations and tax laws (GSTIN: 09AAGCF7185A1ZL, PAN: AAGCF7185A).

Our Mission

To help businesses in India, Saudi Arabia and the UAE run more efficiently with practical AI and automation — custom systems that save time, capture leads and reduce manual work.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted automation partner — known for quality, reliability, and innovation — and to power the digital transformation of 10,000+ businesses by 2030.

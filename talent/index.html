Common questions

Can a sales fresher with AI training experience apply?

Yes. Select AI Chatbot QA & Training and describe your skills. Our owner evaluates each candidate; a sales application is separate from a Talent application.

Do I have to pay to register?

No. Talent registration and the sample assessment are free. No deposit or purchase is required.

Will I receive a salary or guaranteed work?

No. Project availability is variable. Any paid assignment requires owner verification, a written offer and your acceptance.

Can I access real customer conversations?

Not by default. New members see only synthetic practice material. Approved workers only receive explicitly authorised, scoped tasks. Client data remains private.

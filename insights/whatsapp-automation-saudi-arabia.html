Build for bilingual customer journeys

Saudi businesses often need the same workflow to work cleanly in Arabic and English. Language should be detected or selected early, while the underlying lead record stays consistent.

A customer can start in Arabic, a sales executive can manage the lead in English, and the system should preserve the full context without forcing either side into a separate process.

  • Arabic + English greeting and qualification
  • Saudi phone-number and location handling
  • SAR proposal examples where relevant
  • Human handoff for sales negotiation
  • CRM stages visible to management

Where automation creates the most value

Useful Saudi workflows include enquiry qualification, quotation follow-up, field-sales reminders, appointment booking, service status updates, internal approval routing and customer support. Manufacturing and distribution businesses can also connect customer communication with stock, order and collection workflows.

Do not automate sensitive approvals

Money movement, legal commitments, final discounts and irreversible actions should remain human-approved. Automation is strongest when it prepares the information, reminds the right person and records the decision.

A practical rollout

Start with one bilingual lead funnel, prove response and follow-up reliability, then connect deeper systems such as CRM, ERP, inventory or service operations. This reduces risk and makes ROI easier to measure.

Frequently asked questions

Do you need a Saudi office to deliver the automation?

Not necessarily. Freshtiq delivers software remotely from India and can support English + Arabic workflows for Saudi clients.

Can the same WhatsApp flow support Arabic and English?

Yes. The interface can switch language while the lead data and workflow remain unified.

Can it connect with an ERP?

Yes, if the ERP exposes a usable API or another controlled integration method.

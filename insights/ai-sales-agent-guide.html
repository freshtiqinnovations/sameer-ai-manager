An AI sales agent is a workflow, not only a chatbot

A useful sales agent needs memory, lead stages, business rules, tools and a clear handoff policy. It should know what information it can answer, what it must verify and when a human must take over.

Best tasks to automate

Repetitive tasks are the safest starting point: instant acknowledgement, qualification, FAQ lookup, meeting collection, lead scoring, reminders and summaries.

  • Capture requirement and contact details
  • Answer approved product/service questions
  • Score urgency and fit
  • Schedule or request a human callback
  • Summarise the conversation for the sales executive
  • Create follow-up tasks and reminders

Tasks that should stay human-approved

Final pricing exceptions, contractual commitments, payments, refunds, sensitive personal data and irreversible actions deserve a human gate. This keeps automation useful without allowing a language model to make business-critical commitments.

How to evaluate an agent

Test it with real-world messy inputs, not only perfect demo prompts. Measure correct routing, false claims, follow-up completion, response time and human takeover quality. A good agent should fail safely when uncertain.

Frequently asked questions

Can an AI sales agent close sales automatically?

It can support much of the journey, but the right autonomy level depends on the product, price, risk and business rules.

Can it work with WhatsApp?

Yes, through an official WhatsApp integration and a backend that manages lead state and approvals.

Does it need a CRM?

For reliable follow-up, a CRM or structured lead store is strongly recommended.

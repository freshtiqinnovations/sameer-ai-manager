CRM Follow-Up Automation

Turn every enquiry into an owned, trackable next action so leads do not disappear after the first message.

Most lead loss happens after capture

A form submission or WhatsApp message is not a sale. The lead must receive a response, be assigned to someone, move through a clear stage and have a next action. Without that structure, marketing spend and organic enquiries disappear into chat history.

Minimum CRM fields

You do not need a complicated CRM to start. A useful record needs the person, requirement, source, stage, owner and next follow-up time.

  • Name and contact
  • Requirement/service
  • Country or sales territory
  • Lead source and UTM campaign
  • Stage: New, Contacted, Qualified, Proposal, Won/Lost
  • Owner
  • Next follow-up date/time
  • Conversation or activity history

Automate the gaps, not the relationship

Automation can create the lead, alert the owner, remind overdue follow-ups, draft a response and summarise history. A human can handle negotiation and relationship-building.

Metrics that reveal the problem

Track new leads, time-to-first-response, percentage contacted, qualified rate, proposal rate, win rate and overdue follow-ups. Those metrics show where the funnel leaks.

Frequently asked questions

Can this work with a spreadsheet?

Yes for an early-stage team, as long as ownership and next-action fields are enforced. A database/CRM becomes more useful as volume grows.

Can website and WhatsApp leads go into the same pipeline?

Yes. That is usually preferable because it gives one view of the customer journey.

What should trigger an automatic reminder?

A lead with no completed next action by its follow-up time is a simple and useful trigger.

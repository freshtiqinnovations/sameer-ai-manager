How to read this portfolio

Three labels, three different levels of proof.

Internal Freshtiq Build

A real system built and operated internally. It demonstrates engineering capability, not a third-party customer outcome.

Workflow Prototype

A concept or working model built to demonstrate how a business process can be structured and automated.

Implementation Evidence

Technical or workflow evidence showing a real feature, flow or system behaviour without inventing performance claims.

Customer Result

Only used when an actual customer deployment and result can be supported. We do not apply that label without evidence.

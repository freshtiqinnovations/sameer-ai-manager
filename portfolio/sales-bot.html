💬 Freshtiq Sales Bot

AI-assisted WhatsApp sales workflow for enquiries, qualification, follow-up and human handoff when needed.

📅 Built: Jun 2026 🧪 Status: Internal Freshtiq Build 🏭 Industry: AI Automation 💬 24/7 automated sales flows

🎯 The Problem

The sales workflow needed faster enquiry handling, consistent qualification, structured lead records and a reliable handoff path when a customer required human judgement. The goal was to reduce repetitive manual replies without removing human oversight from important decisions.

💡 Our Solution

An AI-assisted WhatsApp sales system that can answer approved product questions, capture requirements, share configured pricing, prepare onboarding steps and hand off to a human when negotiation or approval is required.

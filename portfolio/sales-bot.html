THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

Fast replies are not enough if the lead still gets lost.

The sales workflow needed consistent enquiry handling, structured qualification, reliable follow-up, CRM history and a clear handoff path when commercial judgement or approval was required.

Response consistency

Approved information can be delivered quickly without forcing staff to repeat the same answers all day.

Lead structure

Human control

Negotiation, exceptions, payment decisions and other sensitive actions can stay approval-gated.

Tracked conversion

🏭 El Salama ERP

Dual-bot ERP workflow for a Saudi radiator factory, designed to give customers and staff faster access to order, inventory and dispatch status.

📅 Built: May 2026 🧩 Status: Concept / Workflow Prototype 🏭 Industry: Automotive / Manufacturing 🏭 Factory ERP on WhatsApp + Telegram 📍 Saudi Arabia

🎯 The Problem

The workflow addressed slow manual status checks, repeated order/inventory questions and fragmented factory tracking. The goal was to give customers and staff one structured path for order, inventory and dispatch information.

💡 Our Solution

A dual-bot system: one Telegram bot for customers (order tracking, complaints, inventory lookup) and one internal bot for the factory team (order management, dispatch, inventory alerts). Both connected to the same database.

👥

Customer Bot

Order status, inventory lookup, complaint submission, delivery tracking. In Arabic + English.

🏭

Factory Bot

Internal order management, inventory alerts, dispatch tracking, staff notifications.

🗄️

Shared Database

Both bots read/write to the same SQLite DB. Instant sync between customer and factory.

🤖

AI Support

Ollama-powered AI for complex queries. Handles Hindi, English, Arabic.

📋

Admin Dashboard

Telegram-based admin panel. View orders, manage inventory, approve dispatches.

🔔

Real-Time Alerts

New order → factory notified. Dispatch ready → customer notified.

What happens behind the scenes

1
Order or booking captured
Items or table request taken from the customer through the menu channel you use (for example QR menu or a supported WhatsApp channel).
2
Structured order record
Items, quantities, order type (delivery/pickup/dine-in) and notes written to a clean order record.
3
Kitchen / order dashboard
Staff see the order and move it through a clear status flow instead of scattered chat messages.
4
Customer status updates
Order received → preparing → ready → out for delivery, using supported channels with consent.
5
Human-controlled money
Prices, discounts, refunds and payment handling remain with your staff and your payment setup — the assistant does not invent or approve them.
Outcome: fewer missed orders during peak hours and a consistent status trail for staff and customers.

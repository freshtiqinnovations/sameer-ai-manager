What happens behind the scenes

1
Enquiry answered instantly
Service questions and timing questions get a reply without waiting for the front desk.
2
Booking details collected
Service, preferred stylist, preferred day and time, and contact details with consent.
3
Availability or approval
If the salon calendar is integrated, a slot can be confirmed. Otherwise the request stays pending until staff confirm.
4
Reminder and reschedule
Reminders and change requests handled through supported channels with approved templates where required.
5
Repeat-visit follow-up
Optional follow-up so regular customers are not forgotten — only where consent allows.
Outcome: fewer missed enquiries and a tidier appointment book, while staff keep control of pricing, offers and exceptions.

What happens behind the scenes

1
Instant reply to the enquiry
Respond while the patient is still on the chat, instead of leaving the message for later.
2
Capture the appointment request
Service or department, preferred doctor/reception route, preferred day and time.
3
Availability check or staff approval
If a reliable calendar integration exists, the slot can be confirmed. If not, the request is clearly marked as pending staff confirmation.
4
Reminder workflow
Reminders are sent through supported channels with appropriate consent and approved templates where required.
5
Human stays in control
Reception or clinical staff handle exceptions, urgent cases and all medical judgement.
Outcome: fewer lost booking requests and a cleaner handoff to reception — without the assistant making medical or clinical decisions.

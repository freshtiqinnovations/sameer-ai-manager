What happens behind the scenes

1
Candidate enquiry answered
Applicants get an immediate acknowledgement instead of waiting days for a reply.
2
Structured intake
Role, experience, location, availability and contact details collected in a consistent format.
3
Standard screening questions
Only pre-approved, job-relevant questions are asked. The system records answers; it does not score or filter people out.
4
Interview scheduling request
Preferred slots are collected and either matched to recruiter availability or left pending confirmation.
5
Human decides everything
Shortlisting, rejection and hiring decisions stay entirely with your team. Freshtiq does not build automated hiring decisions.
Outcome: a tidier applicant pipeline and faster first response, while every hiring decision stays human.

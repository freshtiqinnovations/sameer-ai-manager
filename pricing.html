Business Website vs E-commerce Website

Business Website

Marketing/service pages, lead capture, contact/booking request, analytics foundation and automation integrations where specified. No transactional cart/shipping system unless written.

Catalogue Website

Business website plus structured products/services and enquiry flow. A catalogue is not automatically a transactional checkout store.

Transactional E-commerce

Product catalogue plus cart/checkout, payment gateway, order logic, shipping/tax/admin requirements as explicitly scoped.

Chatbot, Inquiry and Booking are different outcomes

Chatbot

Answers: FAQs, service knowledge, basic sales/support questions and human handoff.

Inquiry

Captures & qualifies: contact, need, source, urgency, stage, owner and CRM-ready fields.

Booking

Schedules: service, preferred date/time, availability check when integrated, confirmation or staff approval, reminders.

Combined Business Bot

Chatbot + Inquiry + Booking in one scoped customer journey, with shared context and human handoff rules.

Why India, Saudi and UAE prices are not a simple FX conversion

These are separate published regional starting-price lists, not the same INR amount converted at a daily exchange rate. Regional proposals may differ in language QA, meeting/support handling, provider setup, invoicing/admin requirements or other delivery work. Higher regional pricing does not automatically mean extra features — only the written proposal defines what is included. Use the currency converter for an indicative currency view, not to recalculate a regional package.

Recurring ownership costs — what to check

Freshtiq build cost

One-time or milestone project charge stated in the proposal.

Hosting / VPS / domain

May be customer-owned, managed by Freshtiq, or provided by a third party. Recurring amount depends on the chosen infrastructure.

WhatsApp / messaging

Meta/provider template, conversation or platform fees may apply. Provider pricing can change and is not silently bundled unless your proposal says so.

AI usage

Model/API usage may be variable if a cloud AI provider is used. Local/self-hosted designs have different infrastructure costs.

Licences / gateways

Payment gateways, email/SMS, SaaS tools or other licences are third-party costs unless explicitly included.

Optional managed support

Ongoing monitoring/updates are a separate service unless your written scope includes them.

Your proposal must state: exact modules · integrations · users/roles · dependencies · exclusions · hosting/provider ownership · recurring fees known at quote time · acceptance criteria · handover items · exact support window · change-request process.

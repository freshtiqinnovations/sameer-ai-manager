💇 Salon & Beauty Automation

Automate salon appointment enquiries, service questions, reminders and staff handoff with WhatsApp and CRM workflows for India, Saudi Arabia and UAE.

The Problem

Salon teams lose time answering repeated timing, service and appointment questions while staff are busy with customers.

Real Workflows We Can Automate

  • Customer enquiry → service selection → preferred day/time → staff handoff
  • Instant answers for timings, location and common service questions
  • Appointment reminders and reschedule flow for opted-in customers
  • Lead and booking enquiry capture in CRM

Automation Opportunities

  • WhatsApp appointment enquiry assistant
  • Reminder & reschedule workflow
  • Staff notification and handoff
  • Customer enquiry dashboard

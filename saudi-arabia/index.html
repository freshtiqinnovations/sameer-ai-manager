Where automation creates practical value in Saudi Arabia

Clinics and salons often start with appointment requests, reminders and customer history. Travel businesses can structure enquiries, itinerary requirements and quotation follow-up. Restaurants can organise reservations, menu questions and order enquiries. Sales and service teams can stop losing leads between WhatsApp, calls and spreadsheets. Manufacturing and field teams can centralise work status, approvals and reporting.

Freshtiq starts with one measurable bottleneck, documents the scope and handoff rules, tests the workflow in a controlled environment and expands only after the first process is stable. Production WhatsApp messaging should follow consent requirements and use official or supported business tools/providers.

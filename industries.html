The pattern underneath

Good automation keeps the business context attached to every step.

An enquiry should not become a disconnected chat. The useful design connects it to an owner, a status, a next action and the system your team already uses.

01 · INPUT

Customer or staff request

Capture the minimum data required to understand the work.

02 · RULES

Apply business logic

Route by service, location, urgency or another approved rule.

03 · RECORD

Update the source of truth

Write to CRM, ERP, database or another controlled system.

04 · APPROVAL

Escalate decisions

Keep negotiation, exceptions and sensitive actions human-controlled.

05 · STATUS

Keep people informed

Send the right update without making staff repeat the same work.

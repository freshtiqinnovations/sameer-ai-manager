What the Freshtiq Sales Executive programme is

You introduce Freshtiq's products to businesses that benefit from automation, and you earn based on the deals you help bring in and that are actually collected. Freshtiq supports you with training, approved scripts, lead tracking and a clear process — through our WhatsApp assistant and the owner.

Commission-first

You earn on valid, attributed leads that convert and are fully paid. Final commissions are approved by the owner. Rates are confirmed individually in your written programme terms — they are not the Partner/Reseller rates.

Free to apply, no fee

Applications are free. No joining fee. No guaranteed earnings. No purchase or deposit is ever required to become a Sales Executive.

Owner-reviewed

A human owner reviews each application. Approval is never automatic, and onboarding messages are only sent after approval with your consent.

What a Sales Executive actually does

Where and how to find customers

  • Public business information (listings, websites, social pages).
  • Your personal and professional network, and referrals.
  • Permitted LinkedIn and email outreach that follows platform rules.
  • Local networking, events and industry communities.

Ethical prospecting only — no spam, no private data scraping, no bulk unsolicited messaging.

FAQ

Is there a fixed salary?

No. Earn Freshtiq is commission-first. There is no guaranteed salary or income. A fixed-salary arrangement would require a separate, individual written agreement approved by the owner.

Does it cost anything to apply?

No. Applying is free. Freshtiq never asks for a fee, deposit or purchase to join.

Do I need documents like Aadhaar, PAN or bank details?

Not to apply. We never ask for Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, passwords or OTPs during the application. Any payment or payout details would only be handled securely after an approved agreement.

How will I know if I am approved?

The owner reviews each application personally. If approved, you will receive an onboarding message on WhatsApp (only if you gave consent) and guided sales training. Approval is never automatic.

How is commission calculated?

Your individual rate and terms are confirmed in your written Sales Executive agreement after approval. Commission eligibility requires valid lead attribution, qualifying collected revenue and owner approval. Final payouts are owner-controlled.

Is this the same as the Sales Partner programme?

No. Earn Freshtiq is the Sales Executive recruitment and earning programme. It is separate from the independent Sales Partner / Reseller programme and uses different terms.

Privacy & consent

We collect only what is needed to review your application: your name, WhatsApp number, email, city/country, sales background, preferred market, languages, availability and optional CV/LinkedIn. We do not collect Aadhaar, PAN, bank or card details, passwords or OTPs. Your details are used to review and process your application and — with your consent — to send you application updates and, if approved, onboarding and training messages on WhatsApp. You can ask us to stop messaging you at any time.

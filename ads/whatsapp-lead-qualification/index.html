We scope integrations through eligible official WhatsApp Business Platform access or supported providers. Client verification, template approval, customer opt-in rules and messaging fees may apply.

No. It can qualify and route enquiries, while staff handle negotiations, sensitive decisions, regulated requests and final approvals.

Can you connect our existing CRM?

Where the CRM has suitable API, export/import or webhook access, we can scope integration. Requirements and fees are confirmed in writing.

Cost depends on number of workflows, integrations, languages, conversation complexity and provider fees. The assessment is free; project pricing is quoted after scoping.

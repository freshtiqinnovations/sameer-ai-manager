Common questions

What businesses usually ask before starting.

What should I automate first?▼

Start with a repeated process where enquiries are missed, follow-ups are forgotten, data is copied between tools or customers repeatedly ask for status. The free audit is designed to identify that first workflow.

Can Freshtiq connect with my existing CRM or software?▼

Often yes through APIs, webhooks, databases or controlled integration layers. Compatibility is verified during discovery instead of assumed.

Do you build WhatsApp automation?▼

Yes. We design customer-initiated and consent-based business workflows for enquiry capture, qualification, booking, approved notifications, CRM updates and human handoff using supported WhatsApp Business setups.

How are price and timeline confirmed?▼

Published pricing is a starting reference. Final scope, integrations, timeline, payment milestones and responsibilities are confirmed in a written quotation or proposal before project work begins.

How do payments and project tracking work?▼

Approved project payments can be made through the secure Freshtiq PayU checkout. Customers receive a project reference that can be used on the website and supported bot channels to view a privacy-safe delivery status.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does automation cost?▼

One-time development starts at around ₹8,000 for a basic bot and scales with scope (CRM/ERP and full systems typically ₹25,000–₹2,00,000+). Managed support is optional, from ₹3,000/month. Every project gets a fixed written proposal before work starts.

How long does development take?▼

Simple bots usually ship in 3–7 days, most custom systems in 1–4 weeks depending on scope. You get a clear timeline in the proposal and weekly progress updates.

Do I own the source code?▼

Yes. You receive the full source code for the custom work we build for you, with no vendor lock-in. Third-party and open-source components remain under their original licenses, and framework terms are stated in your proposal.

Can you work with Saudi and UAE companies?▼

Yes. We deliver remotely from India for clients across Saudi Arabia and the UAE, with English + Arabic support and local currency examples (SAR/AED) in proposals.

Is support optional?▼

Yes. One-time development includes a short post-delivery support window. Optional managed support plans (from ₹3,000/month) cover monitoring, updates and priority assistance.

Do you integrate WhatsApp?▼

Yes. We build WhatsApp automation on the official WhatsApp Business API and provider platforms, including sales bots, order management, booking and support flows.

Can you integrate with my existing CRM/ERP?▼

Yes. We integrate with common CRMs, ERPs, spreadsheets and existing tools — or build a custom system that centralises your operations.

How does payment work?▼

Milestone-based: typically 50% to start and 50% on delivery for projects. Larger systems are split into agreed milestones. Payments via UPI, bank transfer or gateway, with a formal invoice and receipt.

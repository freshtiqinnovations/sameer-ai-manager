STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Good questions before buying.

Answers about pricing, timelines and payments without sales pressure.

Are published prices final?

The catalogue shows approved India package prices. Eligible fixed-scope packages display the final GST-inclusive amount in checkout; custom work requires a written scope and quotation. Do not pay until you have reviewed that amount.

Do I have to know which technology I need?

No. Describe the problem in your own words and use the service finder above. We can help identify a suitable service and clarify what is included.

Can I pay by bank transfer?

Only after a matching approved INR invoice is issued for the verified Indian company beneficiary. Sending money to an unknown account or QR screenshot is not accepted proof.

Can I speak to a human?

Yes. The website assistant can explain packages, and you can choose human follow-up via the contact form or business WhatsApp.

Can I read terms in Hindi or Hinglish?

Browsing and package guidance are available in three languages. Official binding policies remain in English; request human clarification before accepting any agreement you do not understand.

Will I get a guaranteed delivery date?

A realistic delivery schedule depends on the selected package, available materials, access and written scope. No universal completion date is promised before confirmation.

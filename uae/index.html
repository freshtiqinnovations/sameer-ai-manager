Useful starting points include real estate, travel, clinics, salons, restaurants, professional services and B2B sales teams. The goal is not to add AI everywhere; it is to remove the specific delays that cause missed enquiries, slow quotations and inconsistent follow-up.

Projects begin with a workflow audit, written scope and clear acceptance criteria. Sensitive actions such as payments, contracts, discounts and irreversible external actions remain human-approved unless a future scope explicitly defines safe automation.

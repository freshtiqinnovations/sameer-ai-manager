FAQ

Common questions

Will the website be SEO ready?▼

We build crawlable pages, titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, sitemap and structured content. Rankings still depend on competition, authority, useful content and time.

Can you redesign an existing site?▼

Yes. We can audit the current site and keep what works while improving structure, speed, conversion paths and integrations.

Can forms connect to CRM or WhatsApp?▼

Yes, when the chosen CRM/provider supports the required integration. We document what is automatic and what needs human approval.

No legitimate developer can guarantee a specific Google position. We focus on controllable technical, content and conversion signals.

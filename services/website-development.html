A business website should be a lead system, not only a brochure

Freshtiq websites are designed around the next useful action: call, WhatsApp enquiry, audit request, booking, quotation request or product/service discovery. The site structure should also help search engines discover important pages and help visitors understand what you do without guessing.

Foundation we plan for

  • Mobile-first layout and clear calls to action.
  • Fast static or application architecture suited to the project.
  • Canonical URLs, sitemap, robots directives and structured data where appropriate.
  • Analytics events for key CTAs and lead forms.
  • Secure form handling with spam/honeypot checks and explicit contact consent.
  • Internal links connecting services, industries, markets and useful guides.

When a web app is the better choice

If users need login, dashboards, role-based workflows, payments, inventory, booking management or complex data operations, a custom web application may be more appropriate than a standard marketing site. The scope should separate public SEO pages from authenticated application areas.

FAQ

Common questions

Will the website be SEO ready?▼

We build crawlable pages, titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, sitemap and structured content. Rankings still depend on competition, authority, useful content and time.

Can you redesign an existing site?▼

Yes. We can audit the current site and keep what works while improving structure, speed, conversion paths and integrations.

Can forms connect to CRM or WhatsApp?▼

Yes, when the chosen CRM/provider supports the required integration. We document what is automatic and what needs human approval.

Do you guarantee Google number one?▼

No legitimate developer can guarantee a specific Google position. We focus on controllable technical, content and conversion signals.

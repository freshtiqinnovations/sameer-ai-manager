What your simple CRM can include

The exact features depend on the written quotation and your existing tools.

  • Lead inbox

    Capture enquiries from a website form or approved integrations.

  • Customer records

    Contact details, enquiry notes, source and status with access controls.

  • Sales pipeline

    New enquiry, contacted, quotation sent, won or closed.

  • Follow-up tasks

    Next-contact date, assigned owner and reminders.

  • Basic reporting

    Enquiry counts, stage breakdown, follow-up status and CSV export.

  • Optional integration

    Approved WhatsApp notifications or APIs when available and scoped separately.

Clear boundaries before we start

This is a simple CRM and lead-tracking service. Factory production ERP, complex inventory accounting, payroll, hospital records, legal tax filing and guaranteed sales leads are not included.

We confirm the number of users, integrations, hosting, ownership, data migration, testing, support and final price in a written scope. Pricing-page amounts are starting estimates, not an instant binding quotation.

How a project works

  1. Tell us the lead sources and follow-up problems.
  2. Approve a feature list, data fields, timeline and quote.
  3. We build a restricted test environment and demonstrate sample workflows.
  4. Approve testing, ownership and delivery details before launch.

BEFORE YOU BUY

Setup, ownership, recurring costs and limitations

Setup requirements

We first confirm the business number/account setup, approved use case, message direction, languages, lead fields, human handoff rule and any CRM/calendar/API access needed.

Provider ownership

Production WhatsApp messaging should use official or supported business tools/providers. The written scope states which account/provider is customer-owned and who is responsible for provider approval.

Recurring costs

Meta/provider messaging fees, AI usage, hosting/VPS, phone-number/provider plans and optional managed support can be recurring third-party or support costs. They are separate unless explicitly included.

Integrations

CRM, ERP, Google Sheets, calendars, payment links, webhooks and APIs can be integrated when access and technical compatibility are verified during discovery.

Consent & templates

Customer-initiated and opted-in journeys are preferred. Business-initiated messaging may require approved templates and must follow applicable WhatsApp/provider rules.

Limitations

Automation cannot guarantee sales, provider approval, message delivery, a third-party API's uptime or a customer's response. Pricing, contracts and sensitive decisions can remain human-approved.

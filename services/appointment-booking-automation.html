Appointment Booking, Reminder & Follow-Up Automation

Automate appointment enquiries, slot collection, confirmations, reminders, rescheduling and staff handoff for clinics, salons, consultants and service businesses.

The business problem

Booking requests get lost when customers message at busy times, staff answer late or reminders are manual. Booking automation keeps the process structured while leaving exceptions and sensitive cases with people.

Common use cases

  • Website or chat booking enquiries
  • Service and preferred-time collection
  • Calendar or staff routing
  • Confirmation and reminder workflows
  • Reschedule/cancel requests
  • Post-appointment follow-up

What Freshtiq builds

  • Booking questions matched to your service
  • Availability integration where supported
  • Customer details and consent capture
  • Reminder timing and no-show reduction workflows
  • Human handoff for exceptions
  • CRM/customer history update

Integrations

  • Google or supported calendars
  • Website forms and chat
  • Official WhatsApp messaging tools
  • CRM/customer database
  • Staff notifications
  • Payment/deposit links when explicitly included

Industries and international delivery

Especially useful for clinics, dental practices, salons, consultants, repair/service businesses, education, real estate viewings and other appointment-led services.

Projects can be delivered remotely for India, Saudi Arabia, UAE and other international businesses when the required tools and integrations are practical to support. Scope, milestones, owner responsibilities and acceptance criteria are documented before development starts.

How we deliver

Free workflow audit → written scope → milestone build → controlled testing → launch and handover. We prefer the smallest useful automation first, measure the result, then expand only when the workflow is stable.

FAQ

Common questions

Can the system book directly into a calendar?▼

Yes when the calendar or booking platform exposes a reliable integration. Otherwise the workflow can collect the request and route it to staff for confirmation.

Can it send reminders on WhatsApp?▼

Yes for compliant customer-initiated or opted-in messaging using official tools/providers and approved templates where required.

Can customers reschedule?▼

Yes. The flow can collect a new preferred slot and either update automatically or send it to staff for approval.

Can we start with a simple demo?▼

Yes. A focused text or workflow demo can validate the booking conversation before a larger integration is built.

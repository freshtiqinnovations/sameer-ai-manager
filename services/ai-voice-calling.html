Responsible launch checklist

  1. Confirm the customer's operating country, destination numbers, use case and written scope.
  2. Check local operator permissions, approved caller identity, recorded opt-in for outbound promotions and legal privacy/recording obligations.
  3. Choose a compatible calling provider; estimate AI + speech + carrier + number charges and agree a prepaid usage cap.
  4. Train on approved scripts, business FAQ and escalation rules. Test in English/Hindi/Arabic only where verified by the selected voice provider.
  5. Run permitted test calls, verify end-to-end CRM updates, latency, handoff, billing and consent withdrawal before go-live.

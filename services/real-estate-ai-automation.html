What to prepare before we begin

What do we need from the real-estate team?

Two example properties (area, price, type, photos), preferred viewing hours, who approves bookings, current data source (sheet/CRM), and business location. No passwords or OTPs by chat.

Are property photos and calendar synchronisation included in Basic?

No. Basic collects enquiries and preferred viewing times for a person to confirm. Property image automation and an editable listing dashboard start with a specifically scoped Professional implementation. Live calendar availability needs a reviewed Elite/custom integration.

Can we connect our existing WhatsApp Business number?

We assess supported Meta WhatsApp Business Platform onboarding and number ownership for your own business. Do not share login codes. Approval and provider limitations may affect timing and price.

Do I pay now, and is this free for life?

No payment for the initial discussion. After requirements review you receive a written scope, final tax treatment, external recurring fees, milestones and secure payment instructions. Operational API/messaging costs are not automatically lifetime-free.
